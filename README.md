# AI Literacy Inclusivity Index

**AI literacy is agency.**

An interactive professional-development resource for VET educators and institutions. It examines whether people are merely given access to AI or are enabled to understand, question, use and help shape it.

## What the resource includes

- A 15-indicator developmental self-assessment
- Three ecosystem domains:
  1. Learner and community agency
  2. Inclusive education and AI systems
  3. AI literacy in society
- Five maturity levels from **Absent** to **Transformative**
- A live institutional profile and policy–experience reflection
- A five-stage roadmap: **Listen → Map → Enable → Embed → Share & Shape**

## Important note

This is a developmental prototype for reflection and professional dialogue. It is not a validated benchmark or institutional league table.

## Conceptual origin and attribution

**Concept and educational framework: Larry McNutt.**

The resource was stimulated by Economist Impact, *Health drives wealth: the economic impact of health inclusivity* (2025), supported by Haleon. It makes an original educational translation; health findings are not presented as evidence about AI.
