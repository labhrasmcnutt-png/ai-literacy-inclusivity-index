Why this matters

AI literacy is public capability infrastructure

People need more than operational skill. They need the capability to recognise AI, evaluate its influence, exercise informed choice, protect their rights and participate in decisions about its use.

Policymakers should prioritise inclusive AI literacy so that everyone can understand, question and help shape AI-enabled futures.
